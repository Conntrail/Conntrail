The method

Measured in three steps

No black box scoring. Every figure is derived from a signal Conntrail captured.

01

Observe

Your node runs unchanged. Conntrail captures the routing decision, its cost and its latency. It never rewrites output.

02

Perturb

A cheap model, never your agent's model, writes three variants of the input: similar, neutral, opposite. The node re-runs on all four.

03

Measure

Shannon entropy over the four outcomes, the first dimension that flips the route, and the counterfactual path it would have taken.
