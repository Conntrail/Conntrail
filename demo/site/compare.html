Not another trace store.

Langfuse, Arize Phoenix and LangSmith record what happened and help you evaluate it. Conntrail answers a different question: how close was the agent to deciding otherwise? Use it alongside them, not instead of them.

When to reach for which

Complementary, not competing.

Arize Phoenix

Open-source tracing and evaluation built on OpenTelemetry/OpenInference. Great for inspecting runs, scoring outputs, and running experiments on datasets.

Add Conntrail when you need to know whether a routing decision would flip under a paraphrase or a tone change — the number Phoenix doesn't compute.

Langfuse

Open-source LLM engineering platform: tracing, prompt management, evals, datasets and cost dashboards, self-hosted or managed cloud.

Add Conntrail when a trace shows what happened but not how brittle the decision was — Conntrail scores the stability of the decision itself.

LangSmith

LangChain's hosted platform for tracing, dashboards, automations and datasets, with Engine to detect recurring failures.

Add Conntrail when you want a counterfactual — "remove this dimension and the agent takes the other path" — rather than another dashboard.

They record what happened. Conntrail measures how close it was to flipping.
