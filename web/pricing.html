Why pay as you go or credits?

Analysis cost scales with the volume you keep and the analyses you run, not with seats. A credit balance caps spend precisely and tops up when you need it, and it keeps the entry point self serve.

Cost audit reports and AI Act templates are evidence, not legal advice: they show what your system did and what it cost. What you file with a regulator is your call.
