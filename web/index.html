Stability under semantic perturbation

Conntrail tells you why your agent decided what it decided — and how close it was to deciding something else.

Most tracing tools record what happened. Conntrail measures how brittle the decision was: whether a paraphrase, a tone change, or a semantic inversion of the same input would have sent the agent down a different route — and which dimension flipped it first.
