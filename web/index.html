Stability under semantic perturbation

Conntrail tells you why your agent decided what it decided, and how close it was to deciding something else.

Tracing tools record what happened. Conntrail measures how brittle the decision was: would a paraphrase, a tone shift, or an inversion of the same input have sent the agent down a different route? And which dimension flips it first?
